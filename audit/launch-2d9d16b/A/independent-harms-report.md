## Security finding: pending ETH contribution can be submitted twice (High, executed)

**Harm and rights.** R73 makes ETH sent through `depositETH()` or plain transfer irrevocable pool capital, including non-creator contributions. `app/src/App.tsx` `useTx.write` reads the sender's *pending* nonce before asking the wallet to send, stores that earlier number with the returned hash, and does not pass that nonce to the wallet. `app/src/tx.ts` `transferUnresolved` uses the recorded lower nonce when its read RPC cannot find the hash. Once an unrelated lower nonce confirms, the guard returns false even though the wallet's higher-nonce contribution is pending. The dashboard permits a second contribution to the same address.

**Executed sequence.** On my owned local Anvil fork of `https://mainnet.gateway.tenderly.co`, block 26,382,349, an unlocked synthetic account had nonce 11,632. A 0.2356 ETH transfer to one recipient was queued at nonce 11,634; an unrelated zero-value transfer at nonce 11,632 mined, leaving nonce 11,634 pending. The normal wallet node returned the pending transaction and `transferUnresolved` returned true. A separate read transport withheld only `eth_getTransactionByHash` for that hash, reproducing a public RPC that has not learned a wallet broadcast; every successful nonce/balance/receipt answer came from the actual fork. With confirmed nonce 11,633, `transferUnresolved` returned false. A second 0.2356 ETH transfer at nonce 11,633 was then sent; both mined, and the recipient gained **0.4712 ETH**. The extra **0.2356 ETH** is 100% of the intended minimum-opening contribution. The recipient was an EOA to isolate the dashboard guard; this witness does not claim that it executed the Balancer deposit function. The on-chain consequence for a Balancer follows from its payable `depositETH()`/`receive()` and R73's contribution rule. Non-creator contributors have no refund claim, and the creator can claim available surplus. There is no fixed deposit-size cap in this guard.

**Smallest fix.** Record the actual wallet transaction nonce once its hash is available and use that nonce for recovery; if the hash and actual nonce are both unavailable, keep the transfer blocked. Do not clear it based on the pre-wallet pending nonce. Also fail closed when persistent storage cannot save the transfer record: `savePending` currently catches storage errors, while `loadPending` converts them to no pending transfer. Test a wallet-selected higher nonce with a lagging read RPC and storage failure, then a genuine replacement/cancellation.

## Economic/product finding: prominent draw price excludes mandatory fee (Moderate, executed fee; UI mapping reasoned)

`shared/planner.ts` `evaluate()` defines `drawPrice` as FWA's acquisition fee. `app/src/App.tsx` puts it in the prominent “Draw price” / “ETH / draw” display and the “For players” block. The separate FWA randomness fee appears only in the expanded “All numbers” details. At the forked 0.5 gwei basefee and 1 gwei transaction gas price, after twenty actual 0.01 ETH Shapes were stocked through FWA, FWA's real `quoteAcquisitionPrice(1)` returned **0.01385475 ETH acquisition fee + 0.00234 ETH randomness fee = 0.01619475 ETH** paid by the player. The actual amount is **16.89% above** the displayed draw-price figure. At 180 purchases with those fixed quote inputs, the omitted line would total 0.4212 ETH; that multiplication is informational, not a demand forecast or a fixed future fee. R19/O10 measure the all-in player payment, so the lead should decide whether the visible price needs to be all-in or explicitly labeled as excluding the separate fee. FWA's own checkout display was not inspected.
