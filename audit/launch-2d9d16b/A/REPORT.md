## Security findings, ranked by consequence

### S1. Pending ETH contribution can be submitted twice — high, executed

**Sequence.** `app/src/App.tsx:267–277` stores the sender's pending nonce **before** the wallet picks and broadcasts the transaction. `app/src/tx.ts:79–84` falls back to that saved nonce when `getTransaction(hash)` says `TransactionNotFoundError`. A wallet can queue the actual transfer at a higher nonce. After an unrelated lower-nonce transaction mines, the guard sees the saved nonce consumed and returns `false` while the contribution remains pending. On owned mainnet forks, two independent probes sent real ETH through this sequence: 0.01 ETH twice to deployed WETH (real contract balance +**0.02 ETH**) and 0.2356 ETH twice to an EOA (recipient +**0.4712 ETH**). A second same-contract transfer was allowed before the first mined. Only the external hash read was made to fail; all successful nonce, transaction and balance answers came from the fork. Same-nonce speed-up/cancel variants held the guard until mining, then released it, as expected.

**Launch impact and rights.** At the 5 ETH opening amount used in the quiet regime, one accidental duplicate could contribute an extra **5 ETH**. `PoolBalancer.depositETH()` and `receive()` accept a second contribution; R73 makes it irrevocable Balancer capital and available surplus claimable by the creator. Neither fork witness sent into a deployed Balancer, so that last consequence is a source-and-requirement inference, not an executed Balancer deposit. Exposure has no fixed amount cap in this UI. **Smallest fix:** obtain and persist the wallet transaction's actual nonce; never clear on a pre-wallet nonce. When actual nonce and hash status are unavailable, keep the transfer blocked. Demonstration source, exact commands, output and failed setup attempts: [app-report.md](app-report.md) and [independent-harms-report.md](independent-harms-report.md).

### S2. Browser storage and tab concurrency can bypass the transfer guard — high, storage behavior executed; wallet race reasoned

`App.tsx:252–255` maps missing, malformed or blocked localStorage to no pending transfer and silently discards a failed save. Two contexts running the candidate's exact storage helpers against Node's real Web Storage API both read no pending record before either wrote; the second write replaced the first hash, and clearing storage caused both to read no pending record. `useTx.write` checks before wallet submission and saves only after the wallet returns. Concurrent **wallet** submission was not executed. `transferUnresolved` also trusts an RPC's `blockNumber` for the hash without a receipt/canonical-block check, so a dishonest successful answer can clear it; supplying fabricated successful RPC answers is prohibited for this audit, leaving that variant reasoned. `transferKey` correctly separates chain IDs, sender and destination (case folded); changing chain does not erase a record for the original chain, but it also does not prevent a separate-chain transaction. **Impact:** a second 5 ETH contribution has the same R73 disposition as S1; amount and frequency are unmeasured. **Smallest local fix:** reserve a cross-tab intent before opening the wallet and fail closed when storage cannot persist it. An absolute promise after storage deletion or with a lying successful RPC needs a trusted shared transaction source or on-chain idempotency, or a narrower product claim. A browser-only record cannot supply that guarantee. Evidence: [app-storage-race.mjs](app-storage-race.mjs), exit 0 in 0.47 seconds.

### S3. Keeper health loses consecutive failure count at restart — medium, executed

`keeper/keeper.ts:139,158–178,577–604` keeps `failures` only in memory while saving other keeper state. Two failed runs, one process restart, then three failed runs leave `health().ok === true` and HTTP `/health` 200, although the pool has failed **five runs in a row**. Repeated restarts can extend the missed alarm. The probe also covered alternating success/error (correct reset), five errors before any success (503), and drop/re-add counter reset. **ETH impact:** no direct transfer; a pool holding the rehearsal's **1.5 ETH** (A) or **0.5 ETH** (B) may remain unattended, with lost sales unmeasured. **Smallest fix:** persist per-pool consecutive failure counts and clear them only on success or confirmed removal. The actual RPC disappearance/reorg path was not executed. Evidence: [keeper-review.md](keeper-review.md), [keeper-health-probe.ts](keeper-health-probe.ts).
