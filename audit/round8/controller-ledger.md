# Controller asset and authority ledger

This is a source-derived map reconciled against the focused fork/receipt work cited below. Unknown external internals are marked; it is not a universal property proof.

| Transition | Before | Nested calls and control | At rest / unresolved check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Factory creation | Creator controls supplied ETH; factory has implementation address and keeper-admin list | `createBalancer` clones implementation, marks clone, then calls payable `initialize`; clone records creator and factory, checks FWA implementation identity/caps, creates FWA pool, installs purchase operator, grants FWA pool Shapes/Packs approval | ETH sits in Balancer; Balancer owns FWA pool; FWA pool can pull Balancer's Shapes/Packs when listed. Verify all constructor/postflight reads and outside-clone behavior. |
| Curated listing | Creator owns an NFT | Balancer pulls NFT, checks `ownerOf`, approves its FWA pool, calls `listNFT`/`listNFTs` | FWA pool should hold NFT, listing is tracked as active; no ETH moves. Hostile collection can callback during pull/approve. Verify actual FWA custody and failure atomicity. |
| Shape/Pack stock-up | Balancer holds creator ETH | `performUpkeep` pays backing plus live mint fee to Shapes directly or through Packs; minted token reaches Balancer, then `listNFTs` moves it to FWA; Balancer records listing and fee component; `_payKeeper` pays caller | Backing resides in prize token; mint fee has left to Shapes; restocking fee is in listed price/`keeperCredit` accounting and keeper reward is liquid ETH from Balancer. Verify issuer balances, exact pack contents and all gas/fee contexts. |
| Player purchase and settlement | Player holds ETH; FWA holds active inventory | FWA quotes/escrows draw, requests VRF, selects/delivers one listing or holds a failed delivery; FWA may credit owner proceeds and charge its edge/VRF service. Balancer is queried through `canPurchase` | Player may hold prize or refund claim; FWA holds owner proceeds and perhaps a failed-delivery token. Exact ordering, price formula, fee destination and refund rights need real traces. |
| Reconciliation/restock | FWA sequence and owner proceeds exist; Balancer may hold spare ETH | `performUpkeep` reads final acquisition sequence, removes drawn item from tracked inventory, collects proceeds, mints/lists deficits, records restocking credit, then pays keeper | Balancer balance is net of proceeds, mint backing/fees and keeper pay. Test reserves and participant entitlements after every step, including draws in flight and failed delivery. |
| Creator unlisting | FWA holds active prize | Balancer deletes tracking under lock, FWA withdraws token to Balancer, Shapes/Packs redeem burns it and transfers backing to Balancer | Backing should return exactly; paid mint fee and any paid keeper reward are one-way. Verify creator can claim final liquid ETH when closed. |
| Failed-delivery refund/recovery | FWA holds undelivered prize and owes purchaser under its refund rules | Purchaser refunds with FWA after its window; creator calls `recoverRefunded`, FWA returns token; Balancer forwards curated NFT or redeems Shape/Pack to ETH | Player's acquisition fee refund, VRF fee, FWA edge, restocking fee/keeper credit and prize backing must be reconciled separately; one seven-day curated refund and creator recovery path passed; failed Shape/Pack delivery remains untested. |
| Close/reopen and withdrawal | Balancer holds liquid ETH and listed prizes | Creator closes, `requiredReserve` becomes zero, `claimSurplus` can transfer all liquid ETH; `open` recomputes `creatorWork` from missing target units | Check whether fully stocked inventory can reopen without liquid reserve, and whether admitted bursts exceed the spare. |

`depositETH` and direct ETH sends grant the creator the balance under the stated rights; donors have no refund claim. Factory keeper admin can restrict upkeep but cannot spend pool assets. Creator transfer changes only the creator role; Balancer remains FWA pool owner. ENS and page-signature calls do not intentionally move ETH or NFTs but can affect identity/metadata authority.
