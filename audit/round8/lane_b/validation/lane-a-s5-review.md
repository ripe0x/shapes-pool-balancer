# Independent Lane A S5 validation

Reviewed immutable `audit/round8/lane_a/validation/CuratedValidation.t.sol` and `coordinator-attempt1.log` against `src/PoolBalancer.sol` lines 353–363 and 802–810. Disposition: **accepted within its explicit adversarial-collection domain**.

- The adversarial collection is a deployable ERC721-like contract controlled by the pool creator. Its `transferFrom` and `ownerOf` intentionally lie in `FakeEscrow` mode. The actual ERC721 ledger keeps token 1 at the creator contract, while the reported owner advances to Balancer, FWA, and each player. Balancer permits any curated collection other than Shapes/Packs and tests only `transferFrom`, `ownerOf`, and `approve`; FWA receives the false custody report. This is an untrusted asset interface attack, not a stand-in for FWA or Shapes.
- `PLAYER` and `PLAYER2` are asserted code-free and funded as external wallets. Each `inventoryVersion()` is read before `vm.prank`, and the subsequent `acquire` call expression contains no contract pre-read. Both payer deltas are asserted exactly: `0.025199431673280128` ETH each. The selection helper calls real VRF service `rawFulfillRandomWords` as its actual coordinator, then the real FWA pool processes the acquisition. No direct service-sender callback bypass occurs in these tests.
- With `FakeEscrow`, FWA acquisition status `2` and listing status `4` mark successful delivery, although `actualOwner(1)` stays at the creator contract. After seven days, `refundFailedDelivery` reverts with observed selector `0x83ad9f5a`; the test establishes rejection, without identifying that selector's error name. In `FakeEscrowRefund` mode the real ERC721 transfer attempt fails, FWA uses acquisition status `7`, listing status `7` after refund, owner proceeds remain zero, and the player recovers the acquisition fee while losing the `0.002457` ETH VRF fee. This control strengthens the distinction between false successful delivery and actual failed delivery.
- One false sale allocates `0.021659458736457265` ETH owner proceeds. The creator began with `1` ETH, lost `0.01234803145` ETH to setup/fees before the draw (Balancer `0.98765196855` ETH), then received proceeds and `0.01` ETH on unlist. Final creator wallet `1.019311427286457265` ETH, net contract-flow gain `0.019311427286457265` ETH. Two false sales allocate `0.04331891747291453` ETH total proceeds and end at `1.040970886022914530` ETH, net contract-flow gain `0.040970886022914530` ETH. Arithmetic matches assertions/logs. These net amounts exclude gas and malicious collection deployment costs.
- The single-prize pool selects the ghost listing at trial 1 in both runs. The test proves two successive purchases/relistings on a fresh fork and an available repeat path; it does not establish an unlimited run, player demand, or a maker's ability to control VRF selection when other prizes are active. A buyer trusting only standard ERC721 `ownerOf` can see a false ownership report; the underlying token has not moved.

The accepted issue is scoped to creator-listed, dishonest ERC721 implementations. It does not show that a standard compliant collection can duplicate custody or that a buyer of a known collection faces this behavior.
