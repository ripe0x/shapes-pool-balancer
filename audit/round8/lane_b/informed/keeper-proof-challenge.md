# Lane B challenge of controller's keeper-credit induction

Reviewed `audit/round8/keeper-credit-proof.md` against frozen `src/PoolBalancer.sol` and the actual local-fork reward/credit transitions. **Accepted as a source-level nominal payout cap**, with the following scope precision.

- Hidden writers: fresh clone storage starts at zero; `keeperCredit` is written only at `performUpkeep` line 589 and `_payKeeper` line 719. No other source function, including close, unlist, claim, configure or refund recovery, cancels/adds credit. Hence the ratchet after unlisting is real.
- Setup toggles: `wasSetup=creatorWork` is sampled at line 583, before `_fillDeficit`. Credit and reward both use this fixed snapshot. Even if an external callback changes `creatorWork` later, it does not change that call's credit recurrence. The proof's phrase “while `creatorWork` was false on entry” should strictly say **calls whose `wasSetup` snapshot was false**; under the trusted real integration they coincide, but entry-vs-snapshot is not required for the induction.
- Integer casts: `ops≤5` Shape units, `restockFee≤0.0047 ETH`, so the `uint128(ops*restockFee())` cast cannot truncate in one call. Accumulated `uint128` checked addition reverts on overflow; reward subtraction is bounded and checked. No wraparound creates spendable credit.
- Packs: `_fillDeficit` increments `used` by `need * _shapesIn(d)` for both direct Shapes and Packs; each Pack listing price adds exactly `_shapesIn(d) * restockFee` (lines 672, 701–708). `_makeup` contains that many Shapes. Thus pack credit and priced fee use the same unit count in successful calls.
- Fungibility: `Σ outside-work nominal payouts≤Σ fees embedded in prizes listed during outside-work calls` does **not** say those exact fee wei arrived as liquid proceeds. Creator liquidity can front rewards; the later player pays a weighted draw price, not the drawn prize's exact fee. Nor does it prove realized keeper profit, reserve solvency or that unused fee credit remains the creator's after an unlisted prize.
- Opening exception: initial `creatorWork=true` listings **include** restock fees in FWA prices but add no `keeperCredit`; setup callers are paid in full from creator ETH. For the corrected Balanced opening at 1 gwei, 47 Shape units price `47×0.001145=0.053815 ETH` of restock fees with no keeper credit. This is consistent with creator paying opening work and later receiving those priced fee components through weighted FWA proceeds, but it narrows a literal “each listing credits keepers” reading of M2. The fork opening test asserted credit zero.
- External behavior: a reverted listing or payout reverts credit changes, so the induction covers successful source transactions. FWA/Shapes/Packs behavior and the codehash pin are trusted inputs; the proof is not an assurance against a malicious or changed integration.

The local fork `credit-ratchet.json` shows residual credit after a funding prize was unlisted and then spent on **useful** reconcile-only work after close. This challenges the wording “unused fees are creator's,” not the nominal cap. The creator can preempt a later outside keeper by collecting/claiming all liquid while closed, but that is a race/availability choice and was not executed in this exact ratchet trace. The code-free EOA repeat in `credit-ratchet-eoa.json` checked the outsider wallet: 127,746 billed gas ×1.05 gwei cost 0.0001341333 ETH; the old credit paid 0.00021638895 ETH, and the wallet gained 0.00008225565 ETH. This is useful work paid from fungible pool liquidity after the funding prize unlisted, not a transfer of an individual segregated fee coin.
