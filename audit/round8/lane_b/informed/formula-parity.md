## Exact fork parity for one full cycle

The corrected Balanced static model at 1 gwei basefee and 1.05 gwei effective bid has weight `1,911,605,302,676,359,387,351`, weighted listing-price total `40,999,999,999,999,999,998,546,800,000,000,000,000`, owner proceeds `0.021447942178543655 ETH`, acquisition fee `0.022520339287470837 ETH`, VRF quote `0.002457 ETH`, and player total `0.024977339287470837 ETH`. These match `audit/round8/lane_b/economics.stdout-stderr.txt`, the fork `EconomicProbe.t.sol` quote/weight output, and the code-free payer in `keeper-receipts-eoa.json`.

The pinned real-fork full cycle selected one 0.01 ETH Shape, replaced it, then closed/unlisted/retired/withdrew and redeemed the winner token. Model destination arithmetic: `0.021447942178543655` owner proceeds − `0.011` replacement cost (backing+mint fee) − `0.001145` keeper reward = **`0.009302942178543655 ETH`** Balancer liquid delta. The test logged exactly that delta; no-win creator reclaim `3.9238010133 ETH` rose to one-win reclaim `3.933103955478543655 ETH` by the same amount. The test asserted zero final Balancer ETH, exactly 0.01 ETH redeemed by winner, and 48×0.001 ETH cumulative mint fees at Shapes. `cycle-parity.py` asserts those values against the retained log. Its Foundry `vm.prank(PLAYER)` was consumed by a nested `p.inventoryVersion()` pre-read, so the test contract actually funded that purchase; separate code-free local Anvil EOA payment proves the quote and selected prize with a real player payer. Neither proves every FWA refund or repeated cycle.

## Hypothetical Shapes fee changes

No Shapes admin was impersonated. At the actual configured 0.001 ETH cap, a real increase to 0.005 or 0.01 would return `MINT_FEE_ROSE` and halt restocking until creator accepts the new fee through `configure`. `fee-model.py` instead computes a **hypothetical full relisting after acceptance** at 1 gwei basefee, 15% markup and the same 0.3 ETH curated value. A separate `fee-rise.ts` model changes the live fee at step 40 without acceptance and stops demand at step 70. These are analytical/model results, not executed fork transitions.
