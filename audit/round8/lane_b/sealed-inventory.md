## Confirmed economic finding E-B1: reserve can fail target restoration after allowed burst

`requiredReserve()` retains one largest prize plus keeper bounds at a fully stocked target. `claimSurplus()` permits the creator to withdraw down to that amount before a burst. FWA may admit a two-draw batch before either outcome is known. Its payment per draw is the weighted average of inventory prices, while the two actual prizes can cost much more to replace. The reserve rises per unrecorded draw only **after** the earlier surplus withdrawal; it cannot recover the withdrawn ETH.

**Launch Balanced witness:** 20×0.01, 10×0.05, 2×0.1, 1×0.5 ETH Shapes, 7×0.02 Packs and one real BAYC at 0.3 ETH base, all configured/listed with 15% markup. On the pinned fork with 0.001 ETH Shapes mint fee and 1 gwei basefee, the creator claimed the maximum 1.8498010133 ETH and left the specified 0.534 ETH reserve. One real FWA two-draw batch cost the player 0.049954678574941674 ETH; coordinator-delivered words selected the 0.5 and 0.1 ETH Shapes (1144 and 206 candidate words; reachability search, not player control of VRF). FWA accrued 0.042895884357087310 ETH owner proceeds. Upkeep reconciled both, replaced the 0.1 prize, and left the 0.5 prize missing. Liquid Balancer ETH was 0.474750884357087310 ETH; replacing the missing prize alone costs 0.501 ETH plus the call's reward allowance. A second upkeep reverted because it could not afford the first missing size. `admissionStatus()` remained 0, so purchases were still admitted at a mix missing the top prize. `requiredReserve()` now reported 1.035 ETH, too late to prevent the earlier withdrawal. If demand stops, target restoration requires a creator top-up; further paid draws might eventually refill it. Witness: `testBalancedLaunchMixReserveAfterLargeAndMediumWins` in `final-focused.stdout-stderr.txt`.

**Larger-prize witness:** with five 0.01 and five 0.5 ETH Shapes plus the same BAYC, the maximum claim left 0.534 ETH. Two 0.5 ETH wins cost the player 0.066025680788240672 ETH and produced 0.058201600750705402 ETH owner proceeds. Upkeep replaced one; 0.090056600750705402 ETH remained, another 0.5 prize was missing, second upkeep reverted, and admission remained open. Witness in the same focused output. The cheap-only control (20×0.01 Shapes) started with 0.044 ETH reserve; five delivered cheap wins after maximum withdrawal were fully restocked, leaving 0.057297312361475350 ETH. This bounds E-B1 to prize mixes/outcomes where burst replacement cost exceeds pre-burst reserve plus proceeds.

This finding violates the owner's M6 restoration guarantee and the stated target-mix liveness. It does not show theft, an attacker-controlled VRF, a universal insolvent state, or failure for every mix. The exact likelihood over time was not estimated; the fork proves the outcomes are reachable through FWA's real selection and settlement path.
