## Owner rights and money destinations derived independently

- Creator contributes ETH and curated NFTs, chooses target, markup and listed curated value, can close and recover listed/returned assets, and can withdraw surplus while open or all ETH after close. Player pays FWA and receives a selected prize or FWA refund; Shape/pack prize redemption returns embedded backing to its current owner. FWA keeps its edge, VRF router its fee, Shapes fee recipient its mint fee, keeper its bounded reward.
- At rest: creator ETH is in Balancer or Shapes backing of a listed prize; curated NFTs and prize tokens are in FWA custody; a pack's Shapes are in Pack custody and their backing is in Shapes. Won prizes move to player custody and retain redemption rights. Draw proceeds sit in FWA until collected. Keeper reward is debited from Balancer ETH and `keeperCredit`; credit is a budget counter accrued at listing, before player repayment, not segregated cash.
- M1-M8 and creator profit require destination checks across mint, list, buy, failed delivery/refund, reconcile, unlist, redeem, close and withdraw. In particular, a paid but unclaimed FWA proceed, a pending refund, or a nonredeemed pack can survive `close`; `close` alone does not assert all assets returned.

## Integration entrypoints, external calls and callback surfaces

- Constructor checks Shapes denomination ladder and Packs underlying Shapes/minimum. `initialize` checks FWA implementation code hash and factory caps, creates an FWA pool, checks owner/implementation, installs `canPurchase` operator, and grants FWA ERC721 operator approval over Balancer-held Shapes/Packs.
- Curated listing calls an arbitrary collection's `transferFrom`, `ownerOf`, and `approve`, then FWA `listNFT[s]`. The listing functions hold Balancer's nonreentrant lock. Curated withdrawal calls FWA; refunded recovery calls FWA and then arbitrary collection `safeTransferFrom` to creator-selected destination. Untracked NFT recovery requires `ownerOf(tokenId) == Balancer`, but can call an arbitrary collection's owner/transfer code.
- Upkeep calls FWA sequence/acquisition/proceeds views, Shapes `mintBatch` or Packs `createPackTo` (which mints Shapes), FWA `listNFTs`, then sends ETH to keeper `msg.sender`. It holds the nonreentrant lock. Creator-only `configure`, `open`, `close`, and transfer functions have no lock; callback exercise is needed, especially if creator is a contract. FWA alone calls read-only `canPurchase`, which calls FWA and Shapes views.
- Unlisting calls FWA batch withdrawal, then Shapes/Pack `redeem`; pack redemption calls Shapes `redeemBatchTo`. Refunded recovery uses the same redeem path. Balancer's balance delta is the credited ETH. Packs `_create` receives Shapes mint callbacks in its `_minting` window, records custody and value, then mints pack to Balancer with ERC721 receipt callback. Packs admin code examined so far exposes presentation controls; full authority mapping remains to test.
- Deployment preflight checks current FWA implementation address/codehash/batch limit. Postflight reads implementation lock, factory/keeper wiring, Packs/Shapes/ENS wiring, minimum pack value, and nonempty implementation/factory code. No created FWA pool, purchase operator, approvals, live Shapes mint/redeem, or pack custody cycle is read back there.

## Candidate counterexamples and unresolved paths

1. FWA owner proceeds, refund and staging semantics may differ from interface assumptions: confirm status constants 2/7, sequence finality, refunded item custody, direct-owner withdrawal limits, price/weight, proceeds payment, purchase operator stipend and delivery retry. A failed delivery round trip must include FWA refund then Balancer recovery/redeem, with player and creator entitlements asserted.
2. Fee drift and pause: Shapes admin can change mint fee up to its cap and FWA can globally enter withdraw-only. `mintFeeCap` stops upkeep/purchases on a rise, but pricing of already listed prizes and reserve change; exercise rise, fall, reconfiguration, open/close and recovery. Determine FWA VRF service/router capacity and pause authorities via public state/runtime.
3. A Shapes/Pack receipt callback and an arbitrary curated collection or keeper recipient can invoke Balancer public functions. Enumerate reentrant entrypoints and check state-dependent outcomes, including creator contract callbacks. Do not impersonate protocol contracts or fake protocol balances/state.
4. Validate exact Pack makeup, fee forwarding, pack backing, redeem payout and admin limits on a real fork. Confirm `createPackTo` receives exact value and `redeem` sends exactly backing after FWA unlist/refund.
5. Challenge the intended economics: creator fronts mint and keeper fees before wins, some may never be recovered; repeated keeper-only work can consume prior credit; closed pools may hold pending proceeds or refunded prizes; FWA/VRF fee changes can make expected profit negative despite accounting conservation. Use launch-scale target and full round trip.
6. Readback gap: deployment checks no FWA/Shapes pause/VRF fee/capacity and does not exercise live clone creation. `initialize` checks FWA caps but deployment only checks max acquisitions; determine whether deploy can succeed yet first pool creation or upkeep fails.
