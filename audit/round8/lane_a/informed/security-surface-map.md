## Destination and release-relevant open paths

At rest, Balancer liquid ETH belongs to creator subject to the stated reserve; FWA holds unearned acquisition escrow and unsettled/refund liabilities, Shapes holds redemption backing and its mint fees, Packs holds component Shape tokens, FWA holds curated/Shape/Pack listed tokens, and winner holds a delivered token. Keeper credit is a cap on future Balancer payouts, not segregated ETH. The current reserve arithmetic fails both F-A1 (creator close/drain/reopen) and E-B1 (two high-cost wins after allowed maximum claim); neither implies listed token backing vanished. F1 shows FWA listing metadata can remain live without NFT custody for a hostile collection. F4 shows local admission can be open while the router rejects a purchase. Q-R8-008 shows target shrink does not remove extra live prizes before the creator unlists them.

Highest remaining schema-2 evidence gaps: exact 70% boundary and each forbidden recipient; hostile batch/listing `approve`/`ownerOf` callbacks; pending Shape/Pack refund, retry and recovery; contract keeper/ETH/NFT recipients; full FWA admin and Shapes fee-authority transitions; ENS registrar changes; Pack renderer/admin effects; long-horizon mixed creator/keeper credit and split-batch upkeep; recent-block fork and genuine Chainlink callback timing; production deployment/runtime correspondence. No current negative result is extended to these paths.
