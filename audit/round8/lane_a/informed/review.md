## Main independent witness and M8 rule challenge

`BalancedBurst.t.sol::testActualPayerTwoWinReserveAndGate` independently reproduced E-B1 at realistic 1 gwei basefee and 1.05 gwei gas price. The creator's maximum surplus claim was **1.8498010133 ETH**, leaving the originally reported **0.534 ETH** reserve. The actual `PLAYER` EOA paid **0.049954678574941674 ETH** for two real FWA requests. Authenticated word deliveries selected 0.5 and 0.1 ETH Shapes in 824 and 129 candidate trials. FWA owner proceeds were **0.042895884357087310 ETH**. After keeper upkeep replaced the 0.1 Shape, liquid Balancer ETH was **0.474750884357087310 ETH** and the 0.5 Shape was still absent. The next 0.501 ETH mint plus the bounded call reward allowance would need **0.50486 ETH**, a **0.030109115642912690 ETH** shortfall. `requiredReserve()` rose to **1.035 ETH**, a **0.560249115642912690 ETH** gap relative to liquid. The next upkeep reverted `NoUpkeepNeeded` because it could make no progress; it did not attempt an unaffordable mint. A creator top-up or sufficiently favorable later paid draws can restore the target; neither is guaranteed if demand stops. The creator can force the pre-burst maximum claim, and a player can force the two-draw issuance, but neither controls mainnet VRF words. The witness is an attainable outcome, not a forced choice of prizes.

M8 arithmetic gave **9033 bps** while two draws were in flight and **9991 bps** after the high prize remained missing, both above the 70% threshold. `admissionStatus()==0` even though the 0.5 ETH prize count was zero. This challenges the **weight rule itself**: inverse-price weight assigns the expensive prize little gate weight, so passing the 70% arithmetic can coincide with collapse of the high-prize odds and unresolved target restoration. Exact 70% boundary and consumer expectations remain untested.

`BalancedBurst.t.sol::testTargetShrinkLeavesOldPrizesPurchasableUntilUnlisted` also passed. The creator shrank the target to twenty 0.01 ETH Shapes without first unlisting extra denominations. `creatorWork` stayed false, Balancer admission stayed 0, and live FWA count/weight/acquisition fee stayed unchanged at **0.022520339287470837 ETH**. The actual `PLAYER` paid **0.024977339287470837 ETH** and a delivered VRF word selected the 0.5 ETH Shape, which was outside the new target; upkeep did not refill it. Current `configure` explicitly says smaller targets leave extra prizes listed until `unlistShapes`, so this is an owner-controlled target-mix semantics and product-rule exposure. It is not evidence of unilateral outsider theft. A creator can close or unlist before reopening to avoid selling an extra prize under the new plan.
