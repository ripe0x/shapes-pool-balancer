## Residual leads and gate

1. **Asset and economic findings remain live independently of these detector labels.** F-A1 close/claim/reopen admission with zero liquid and E-B1 Balanced two-win reserve shortfall are real fork witnesses. F1 public seizure and the stricter-route dishonest ERC721 repeated ghost sale show that FWA listing/delivery status can disagree with actual ERC721 custody. The 70% M8 gate can pass despite collapsed odds for high prizes. None is ruled out by a benign local-initialization or imported-library finding.
2. **Callback boundaries remain bounded.** ReentrancyGuard blocks tested nested guarded keeper/claim/NFT withdrawal calls. Creator-controlled ERC721 callbacks can still change markup during listing, with stale or mixed prices. The two High reentrancy alerts involve official FWA/Shapes/Packs calls; no universal negative about callbacks, transient views, fee-admin changes, or all actor contracts is justified. The updateCuratedPrice Medium alert has one real unsettled-draw rollback; the old post-settlement callback bypassed the strict coordinator route and is diagnostic.
3. **ENS is a mutable trust edge.** The live pinned registrar and resolver honored set/clear on the fork, and no asset transfer occurs in setENSName. The registry owner can change; return-value omission allows an authorized call to emit ENSNameSet even if a future registrar returns a bogus node/name, subject to registrar behavior. That remains a metadata-integrity dependency, not a demonstrated unprivileged asset drain.
4. **Imported Solady flags are not target-path findings for this build.** All 24 such IDs refer to functions absent from the call graph above. This is a reachability conclusion for the frozen target, not a statement that the library functions are correct. A future source change calling one reopens it.
