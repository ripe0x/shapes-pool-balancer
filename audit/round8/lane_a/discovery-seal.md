3. The FWA pool must own listed curated NFTs and Shape/Packs. The Balancer grants the pool blanket Shape/Packs approval, so a non-owner must not use FWA listing functions to pull Balancer-held, preexisting tokens. 4. Shape/Pack backing must be held by the live token and return exactly on unlist or refunded recovery. Mint fees accrue to Shapes; Pack custody must name and retain each component Shape. The creator may recover directly sent NFTs; the sender has no refund right under the stated design.

Keeper pay outside creator work must draw from `keeperCredit` originating only in listed-prize restock fees; the creator bears setup work in full. FWA edge and VRF fees are separate one-way player costs.
6. While purchases are admitted, liquid Balancer ETH should meet the restoration/spare/keeper reserve. A player payment sitting in FWA escrow and backing locked in a live token are not liquid restoration funds. Closing, withdrawing, and reopening must preserve this if the reserve promise is unconditional.
7. The 70% gate must account for pending draws at the heaviest possible Shape weight, with curated inventory present. Every step of close → unlist → curated withdrawal → ETH withdrawal should leave no routine-flow balance stranded. Retirement and refunds remain separately reachable.
8. ## Confirmed economic finding: open with a zero liquid reserve

`close()` sets `enabled=false`, making `requiredReserve()` zero. The creator can claim the entire liquid balance, then `open()` on already full prize inventory. `_creatorChanged()` clears `creatorWork` because no units are missing; `admissionStatus()` checks weight and draw state but never whether balance meets `requiredReserve()`. This is an authorized creator sequence that violates the owner's M6 reserve premise and can halt restoration after an expensive prize is won. It does not make the live Shape unbacked; the winner's existing token backing remains at Shapes. It is also visible to ordinary dashboard users as `Open. Players can buy draws. Nothing needs attention.` because status 0 is returned while the reserve is absent.

Pre-exposure real-fork witness at block 26382349: creator of BAYC #1 was funded as an external EOA, deposited 101 ETH into an official factory clone, listed BAYC #1 at 1 ETH, configured and minted one 100 ETH Shape, and paid 0.000587212983582396 ETH creator upkeep reward. The Shape held its 100 ETH backing at Shapes. The creator closed and claimed the remaining 0.998412787016417604 ETH. After reopening: liquid balance 0; `requiredReserve()` 100.034 ETH; `admissionStatus()` 0; `canPurchase(0xBEEF)` true. A funded 0xBEEF EOA then paid 2.081548187824626386 ETH to the real FWA pool, which issued one acquisition. The player payment was FWA escrow, not Balancer liquidity. The test did not deliver VRF words or prove which prize would be won.
