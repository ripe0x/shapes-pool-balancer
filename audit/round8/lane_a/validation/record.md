## Paths and actor/asset ledger

1. **`ownerOf` changes after a real pull.** The collection transfers its token creator→Balancer→FWA, then returns a false owner when the actual internal owner is FWA. FWA listing reverts; the entire listing transaction rolls back: internal and reported owner are creator, active FWA listings and Balancer IDs are zero, and Balancer still holds the exact 1 ETH deposit. This proves rollback for this lie timing, not honest custody for arbitrary hostile ERC721s.
2. **False pull alone versus full dishonest custody.** A no-op creator→Balancer transfer plus `ownerOf` lie fails at the collection's standard approval; no listing persists. If the collection also no-ops Balancer approval and Balancer→FWA transfer, FWA creates active listing ID 1 while the internal token owner remains the creator and public `ownerOf` reports FWA. Balancer `admissionStatus()` is 0 after actual Shape upkeep. The untracked internal asset is controlled by the hostile collection, so no generic custody guarantee follows from the FWA listing status.
3. **Approval revert/callback.** A collection `approve` revert rolls the complete listing back, with creator token, 1 ETH deposit, zero listing/IDs and zero markup unchanged. During a successful separate listing, `approve` calls back into creator-controlled `configure(10_000,1)`. The markup becomes 100%, while the already computed FWA price remains exactly 0.1 ETH and local admission is 0 after upkeep. This is the bounded F3 stale-price path. It does not assert that all callback orders are safe.
4. **Ghost delivery with standard ERC721 transfer.** The collection fakes escrow custody but uses standard transfer for FWA→player, which reverts because internal owner remains creator. A code-free EOA player funds a draw with 25,199,431,673,280,128 wei; coordinator words reach the ghost (one candidate trial). FWA acquisition enters status 7 and listing status 6 pending delivery; owner proceeds remain zero. After seven days, player calls real `refundFailedDelivery(ghostId)` and `withdrawAcquisitionRefund()`; listing becomes status 7. Player ends exactly **2,457,000,000,000,000 wei** below the pre-purchase balance, equal to the VRF fee; acquisition fee returns. Internal token owner is still creator and Balancer liquid is unchanged by the refunded draw. No gas costs are included in Foundry balance arithmetic.
5. **Ghost delivery also faked.** The same hostile collection additionally no-ops FWA→player transfer and reports `ownerOf` as player. Actual internal owner remains creator, but real FWA acquisition status is **2 fulfilled** and listing status **4 delivered**. First code-free player EOA spends **25,199,431,673,280,128 wei** (22,742,431,673,280,128 acquisition fee plus 2,457,000,000,000,000 VRF fee), obtains no actual ERC721 ownership, and cannot transfer the token. FWA allocates **21,659,458,736,457,265 wei** owner proceeds, which a keeper reconciles into Balancer. Balancer before draw: **987,651,968,550,000,000 wei**; after upkeep: **1,009,311,427,286,457,265 wei**. Creator closes, unlists its one remaining Shape, and claims **1,019,311,427,286,457,265 wei**; Balancer ends at zero. Relative to the creator's 1 ETH initial deposit, its wallet is **19,311,427,286,457,265 wei** above principal, excluding gas and NFT market value. At seven days, real `refundFailedDelivery(ghostId)` from the correct buyer reverts selector **`0x83ad9f5a`** with listing status 4, rather than entering the failed-delivery refund path. The selector is recorded without assigning an unverified error name.
6. **Same internal token sold twice.** After first fulfilled fake delivery and proceeds reconciliation, creator lists the same internally held token again as a fresh FWA listing. A second code-free player EOA funds and reaches that listing through the actual coordinator route. Both acquisitions are status 2 fulfilled, both listing IDs status 4 delivered, both seven-day refund calls revert `0x83ad9f5a`, and the internal owner stays creator. Each player spends **25,199,431,673,280,128 wei** and receives no actual token. Each draw credits **21,659,458,736,457,265 wei** owner proceeds, which keeper reconciliation moves to Balancer. After close, 0.01 ETH Shape backing return, and claim, creator wallet is **1,040,970,886,022,914,530 wei**, **40,970,886,022,914,530 wei** above its 1 ETH initial deposit, excluding gas and NFT market value; Balancer ends zero. First and second selected words both occurred in one candidate trial in the test's search. This shows repeatability conditional on the dishonest collection and favorable coordinator words. The creator and player do not control the real VRF coordinator's random words; this test demonstrates a reachable outcome, not their ability to force it.

The player ETH loss and creator proceeds are exact for the pinned fork, one 0.1 ETH curated listing, 1 gwei basefee, 1.05 gwei tx gas price, and the tested malicious collection. FWA's public owner check trusts an ERC721 implementation that can lie; the internal ownership assertion is available only because this counterparty exposes its real ERC721 `_ownerOf` ledger for the test. Another collection's behavior or offchain enforceability is outside this proof. The two-draw test does not value the NFT or include transaction gas in the player/creator balance deltas.
