# Nominal keeper-credit invariant, frozen source

This is a source-level induction for **nominal ETH sent to outside keepers by `performUpkeep` while `creatorWork` was false on entry**. It is not a proof of keeper profit, of reserve solvency, or of who originally supplied each fungible wei. Reviewed target: `src/PoolBalancer.sol` at commit `2ac7a7e7b965d1798729068566c026128a4ba4b9`.

Let `C` be `keeperCredit`, initially zero in a fresh clone. Let `A_t` be the sum of restocking-fee components embedded in Shape/Pack prizes listed in a call `t` that began with `wasSetup=false`. Let `R_t` be ETH actually sent to the caller in that call. The source writes `keeperCredit` only at lines 588 and 719; an exact search of the frozen source confirms no other writes. `_fillDeficit` returns `ops = Σ need_d × shapesIn(d)` for successfully minted/listed prizes, and each listed price is `_prizeBase(d,fee,markup) + shapesIn(d) × restockFee()` at lines 672 and 702. Therefore `A_t = ops × restockFee()` for that transaction's listing block. FWA listing failure reverts the entire transaction and neither fee nor credit survives. If `wasSetup=false`, line 588 adds `A_t` to `C`; if `wasSetup=true`, no credit is added and creator-started work is paid separately.

At lines 713–720, `_payKeeper` computes `raw = min(measuredGas,3.2M) × min(tx.gasprice,block.basefee+0.05gwei,5gwei) +0.0005ETH`. With `wasSetup=false`, it sets `R_t = min(raw,C_after_add,address(this).balance)`, returns zero without a transfer if that is zero, otherwise subtracts exactly `R_t` from `C` and sends exactly `R_t` to `msg.sender`. Solidity 0.8.28 checked addition/subtraction means an overflow/underflow reverts the whole call rather than creating an unaccounted payment. For all successful non-creator-work calls, `C_{t+1}=C_t+A_t-R_t`, with `C_t≥0`. Summing from initialization gives `ΣR_t = ΣA_t-C_final ≤ ΣA_t`.

This establishes the requested lifetime **credit cap** for the current source, including split batches, overbids, gas-limit inflation attempts, EVM refunds and refunds of *player* deliveries: none of those paths writes credit except by listing Shape units. It does **not** establish that the player who won a particular prize repays that prize's fee (FWA charges a weighted average), or that credited fees were collected as liquid ETH before a keeper is paid (creator capital fronts them). Credit is not tied to a particular prize; an unwon prize can be unlisted while residual credit funds a later useful reconciliation. It also does not cover the full uncapped payout for calls that began with `creatorWork=true`, because those are expressly creator-started work. Reentrancy and external protocol behavior do not add an alternate credit writer in this source; a malicious external call can revert or alter ordering, but a successful transaction must obey the checked storage writes. The trusted-source assumption for FWA/Shapes/Packs and current compiler semantics remains explicit.

Real-fork tests and code-free EOA receipt evidence in `lane_b/sealed-inventory.md`, `lane_b/informed/keeper-receipts-eoa.json`, and `lane_b/validation/credit-ratchet-router.json` check sample transitions, not the induction itself. A later actual coordinator-route [failed-delivery receipt](lane_b/validation/refund_gas/refund-gas.json) demonstrates the proof's cash-source limit: after a creator claimed Balancer balance down to zero,0.00017954115 ETH credit persisted; a new0.001 ETH deposit was the sole liquid source for the next outside-keeper reward. The historical-credit inequality remained true while the owner's money-destination rule failed. The proof should be independently challenged for hidden writers, setup toggles within a call, and whether `ops` counts all listed pack Shape units. No claim about keeper **profit** follows without billed gas × effective price and wallet balance reconciliation.
