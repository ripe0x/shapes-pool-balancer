# `sim/flows.ts` formula and fork review

This is a controller review after discovery seals. The production simulation is a model, not a fork execution. All comparisons below use frozen source commit `2ac7a7e7b965d1798729068566c026128a4ba4b9`; Lane B's 150-row scenario matrix and bounded fork parity are linked in the [report](report.md).

| Model site | Contract or integration site | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `priced`, lines 49–58 | `PoolBalancer._prizeBase`, `_fillDeficit`, `restockFee`; curated `_priced` | Shape/Pack base, mint fee, backing markup and listing-time restock fee formulas match integer structure for the `gasFee` policy. Curated price uses the markup at listing. Other funding policies are counterfactual, not current contract behavior. |
| `targetOf`, 76–79; `designPrice`, 97–108 | `configure`, `shared/design.ts` | Mix is derived at initial conditions. A later markup/mint-fee change does not recompute target weight in the model; contract `configure` does recompute it. Later scenario odds are therefore not exact contract outcomes. |
| `tw`, `evNow`, `feeNow`, 103–105 | FWA inverse-price weights, edge | Integer weighted-price structure matches the static pinned-fork acquisition-fee parity reported by Lane B. The model does not include the live FWA price/inventory version, nested staging and refund state as actual contract calls. |
| `reserve`, 120–130 | `PoolBalancer.requiredReserve`, 738–761 | The restoration, largest-prize and capped-keeper terms have the same shape, but the model passes the **live** mint fee; contract uses `max(live Shapes mint fee, mintFeeCap)`. The model understates reserve when a fee falls below the creator-accepted cap. A separate real Shapes-admin fork set the fee to0.005 and0.01 ETH and observed `requiredReserve()` rise and purchases pause until reconfiguration ([record](lane_a/validation/mint_fee/record.md)); a fee falling below cap remains a source-derived divergence. Its `pending + unreconciled` counts are an abstraction of FWA `lastIssuedSequence - reconciledSequence`. |
| `gate`, 141–153 | `admissionStatus`, 542–553 | Weight uses listing price less restock fee, matching stored `Item.base`; it subtracts the fixed 0.01-ETH maximum per unreconciled draw. It models curated presence with a set and FWA busy state with pending length. It does not execute FWA's live purchase hook, version cap or price slippage. |
| `request`, 155–163 | FWA purchase and VRF service | `VRF = 0.0003 ETH` at line 15 is constant, while the live router's fee depends on `tx.gasprice`. At the pinned fork and 1.05 gwei, the real fee was **0.002457 ETH**. Model full player cost is understated by 0.002157 ETH per draw in that state. `metrics.paid` at line 184 omits VRF entirely from printed player price. Other gas prices magnify or shrink the error. |
| `settleAll`, 165–187 | FWA VRF settlement, delivery and refund | Random selection and slippage are approximations. There is no actual collection transfer failure, seven-day refund, FWA owner-claim timing or player refund payment. `refundOwed` is a conservation bucket, not an executed entitlement. |
| `upkeep`, 189–250 | `performUpkeep`, 562–596; `_payKeeper`, 711–726 | Value-order replenishment, credit creation and cap logic resemble the contract. Gas is a fixed estimate from `shared/planner.ts` (line 220), whereas the contract pays measured EVM gas and the keeper pays billed receipt gas. The simulation therefore cannot prove keeper profitability, calibration or refund spread. Its stop rule is a keeper decision, not a contract rule. |
| `check`, 132–139; close loop, 252–279 | M1–M8 at rest and full cycle | The assertion checks global conservation and Shape backing count. It does **not** assert that the liquid reserve is available, fees are in the right bucket, FWA refunds are claimable or designed odds remain. On close, it carries any `refundOwed` rather than paying it; the printed `stranded` sum omits that bucket. It is not a full M7 refund cycle. |

The static Balanced fork formula comparison is a valid narrow parity point; it does not make the 40-seed, 120-step runs on different gas, staging or fee-admin states fork-validated. A complete cheap-Shape cycle passed through the actual coordinator→VRF service→FWA route with player payment, winner redemption, creator unlisting, withdrawal and zero final Balancer balance ([strict-route output](lane_b/validation/router-full-cycle.log)). Earlier direct-service-sender arithmetic remains diagnostic only. The [report's scenario table](report.md) labels model-only quantities. The fixed VRF underquote is a fork-confirmed economic finding against decision support; other model/fork disagreements remain bounded or untested.
