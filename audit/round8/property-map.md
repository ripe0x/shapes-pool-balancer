## Monotone and ratcheting values

`reconciledSequence` and FWA request/issued sequences increase; `pageEpoch` only increases on revocation or creator transfer; retirement is irreversible; Shapes accrued mint fees and FWA/VRF fees leave irreversibly; each listed prize keeps its historical price even after configuration changes; approvals remain until explicitly changed (Shape/Pack FWA approvals are permanent in this source). `keeperCredit` is **not** monotone overall: listings increase it, payouts decrease it, and unlisting does not cancel it. The full time horizon of unused credit and standing approvals, and the owner's reason for retaining them, have not been confirmed. The page and keeper-admin two-step handovers have no token spending permission by source; hostile external contracts and counterfeit outside clones remain separate presentation/custody questions.
