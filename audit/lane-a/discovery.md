## Rights and candidate obligations derived from participants

- Creator controls configuration, curated inventory and liquid surplus. Depositors other than creator have no withdrawal right in the stated product rules; this is consequential for anyone sending ETH/NFTs directly. A pending creator gains those rights only by accepting the transfer.
- Buyer pays FWA, receives the selected NFT on delivery, or holds FWA refund rights after expiry, slippage or failed delivery. The Balancer must not revoke those FWA rights or spend escrow. The buyer's chance of a curated NFT and quoted acquisition fee should reflect live FWA totals at admission.
- Pool/Balancer custody: FWA holds active and pending NFTs; Shapes holds the ETH backing of outstanding Shapes; the Balancer holds liquid ETH and may temporarily hold a Shape during mint/list or withdraw/redeem. The creator may sweep untracked NFTs. Liquid reserve must cover the promised active-plan refill under an admitted batch; outstanding FWA obligations are not liquid Balancer assets.
- Keeper may reconcile and restore, with reward bounded by measured gas, capped reimbursable price and incentive credit. A self-dealing creator/buyer/keeper should not profit solely from cycling a purchase or exploiting refunds; competing keepers can cause duplicate losing attempts but a successful call pays only its caller.
- Factory must not retain clone authority; initialization must bind clone to reviewed FWA implementation, fixed batch cap and own FWA pool. ERC-1271 approval should be creator-controlled, exact digest and epoch scoped. ENS name setting must not become arbitrary external authority.
- These rights are hypotheses from SPEC/OPERATIONS and actor economics, not proof that the product rules are sufficient. In particular, the exact economic entitlement of ETH sent by non-creators is a product decision to review.
