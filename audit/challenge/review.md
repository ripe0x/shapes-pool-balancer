## Further reconciliation and limits

- **Correct lane B's edge premise before reuse.** Its B4 candidate relied on public FWA settings prose saying half the edge goes to the pool owner. The integration assessment of this pinned `StandardPool` source instead finds `ownerProceeds += basePrice`, with the entire `paid − basePrice` credited as protocol fee and routed by `StandardPoolBuyback` to FWAV2Buyback. The source was only partially verified by Blockscout; the implementation runtime hash matches the supplied hash, but full source/runtime reproduction was not done. Under this code, a creator self-purchase does not recover half the edge as owner proceeds. **Reject** the half-edge profit premise for the inspected implementation; retain the broader keeper net-cash question because `incentiveCredit` is only a cap on Balancer-funded ETH, not FWA money transferred to Balancer. A purchase/refund/upkeep net-asset trace remains the deciding evidence for profitability or drain claims.
- The older `docs/SECURITY.md` discusses contract-purchaser lockup of transfer-restricted curated NFTs. It was read only after discovery was sealed. It does not validate or invalidate the new controller's exact frozen-withdrawal trace or prove all hostile-collection outcomes are bounded. The new tests were executed through this lane's Foundry path. No structural gate, stateful invariant run, deployment broadcast, or full source-to-bytecode reconstruction was performed in this challenge.
