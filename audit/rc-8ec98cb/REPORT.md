## Independent asset map and flow breakdown

I derived this from `PoolBalancer`, `BalancerFactory`, FWA and Shapes before comparing the gate: creators deposit ETH and curated NFTs into the Balancer; the Balancer mints Shapes/Draw Packs with backing plus the 0.001 ETH per-Shape fee, and lists those prizes and curated NFTs on FWA. Players pay FWA an acquisition price, 5% edge and fee for randomness. FWA escrows the draw price and either sends the chosen NFT to the player, leaves a player refund credit, or sends owner proceeds to the Balancer. The Balancer collects proceeds, books creator profit, reserves the game's share and pays useful keeper calls; the restocking fee embedded in Shape prices is repaid only when those prizes are won. Closeout stops sales, keeps pay for unresolved draws, unlists/redeems prize backing and returns liquid excess to the creator. FWA edge, randomness service charges and Shapes mint fees do not reverse; R27 accepts those destinations.
