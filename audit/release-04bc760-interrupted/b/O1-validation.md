# O1 discovery and informed validation
Independent discovery test observed 15,097,273 applied refund gas over 180 fulfilled draws, or 83,873 gas/draw. Its diagnostic assertion against 80,000 failed; logs and raw-source candidate remain retained.
Root then challenged the quantity: O1 concerns retained keeper refund gas, not the entire applied refund counter. This message is an informed validation contribution after independent discovery, not blind discovery credit.
Independent reconciliation: for each actual paid call, V1 reward = (measuredGas + 50,000)*price + finalizedDraws*0.00002ETH, provided no cap clips. This identity is asserted against actual emitted KeeperPaid and return amounts. Actual billed gas is the immediate low-level lastCallGas used field. Its post-refund meaning is separately demonstrated by the storage-clear witness. Removing explicit service and overhead from actual rewards leaves measuredGas. Retained refund gas under O1's metric is max(measuredGas-billedGas,0), not the entire applied refund counter. Non-measured post-payment execution and intrinsic costs absorb part of the raw refund.
The validated comparison is therefore approximately 60,448 gas/draw, below 80,000; final exact totals are in refund-validation-03.log. The earlier raw-counter excess is withdrawn as an O1 violation. The retained raw evidence remains useful for distinguishing the units, but it does not establish an accepted outcome breach.
