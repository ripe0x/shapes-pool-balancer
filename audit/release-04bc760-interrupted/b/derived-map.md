# Independent money and rights derivation (before builder flow helper)
Creator contributes liquid ETH to Balancer, sets 1-1000 Shape/Pack target and 10-100% markup, supplies arbitrarily valued curated NFTs. Owner-only FWA inventory belongs to Balancer. Existing listing prices cannot change except creator curated repricing while no pending acquisition. Creator can change target, close, recover/unlist and claim above dynamic requirement or booked profit. No other party may name creator payout destinations.
Pool liquid ETH is distinct from actual Shapes backing held by Shapes (or Shapes inside Packs) and listed future fee expectations. Mint transfers backing plus mint fee irreversibly; unlist/redeem returns backing only. Restock fees are listing price entries, not ETH held. Each current Shape listing has price=backing + actual mint cost + backing*markup + policy surcharge. Curated price has no markup or surcharge.
Buyer pays FWA acquisition fee plus VRF fee; VRF fee is forwarded irreversibly to real service. Acquisition fee remains buyer escrow until settlement. Successful delivery splits request-time base expected listing price to ownerProceeds and edge to FWA accruedProtocolFees. Failed delivery holds delivery escrow; only purchaser after 7 days can refund; expiry/slippage refund is pull credit. Balancer cannot claim either buyer bucket. A malicious collection can undo a completed transfer, explicitly accepted under R20.
Balancer collects owner proceeds. While open, current tracked Shape markup above first 10% is booked profit using mean per-listing share. Collections before reconcile retain won entries, concurrent list/unlist changes cause accepted attribution drift. Other cash funds maintenance and replacement. Open/withdrawal retain missing target's mint costs, its bounded work, actual unreconciled draws' current reconciliation budgets, and one bounded operating call. Paid work excludes profit; unpaid work may consume it. Closing drops target/buffer requirement, disabling drops future pay hold. Nothing refunds already paid keeper work, Shapes mint fees, FWA edge or VRF fees.
Keeper receives immutable verified-operation read-only policy quote, min of measured envelope/cached maximum/0.311ETH absolute maximum, creator liquidity and persistent 0.622ETH burst with 0.05ETH per12s refill; caller floor atomic. Policy changes need creator proposal +2days, public activation with liquidity excluding profit; factory default change only new pools, keeper eligibility separate.
Ratchets: FWA sequence, reconciled sequence, policy generation, page epoch, irreversible minted fees and keeper payouts only increase. Former counters are identities/replay protection; latter irrecoverable service costs. Existing historical high-gas listing fees persist until won/unlisted. Staged upper bound can conservatively overstate until active-count synchronization; investigate gate liveness.
