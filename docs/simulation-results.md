# Simulation results

Generated by `npm run sim` (`sim/flows.ts`), 40 runs per row, 400 steps each. Pool: one 0.3 ETH NFT (a fresh one is
listed when it is won) and the Balanced mix at 20 small Shapes (20 × 0.01, 10 × 0.05, 2 × 0.1, 1 × 0.5 ETH Shapes and
7 × 0.02 ETH packs), 15% markup, 0.001 ETH mint fee, 1 gwei basefee, 3 draws a step, keeper every step, the dashboard's
deposit plus 0.2 ETH. Upkeep as on chain: 5 reconciles, 5 Shape units and 4 sizes per call, smallest value first. The
opening stock-up is creator work, paid in full; after it, rewards are capped by the markup earned, and an honest keeper
sends a call only when the reward covers its gas. FWA is modelled from its code: weights 1e36/price, fee = EV × 1.05,
owner proceeds = EV at request, staged listings activated 6 per request, 10% slippage refunds.

**Money flows.** After every step the ledger reconciles to the wei: creator deposits + NFTs listed + player payments =
pool ETH + FWA owner proceeds + FWA escrow + refunds owed + listed prize backing + prizes won + NFTs won + mint fees +
FWA edge + VRF fees + keeper rewards + creator withdrawals. After every keeper call, lifetime keeper pay outside the
opening stock-up is checked against the markup earned (listed price less what the prize cost) on delivered draws. After
closing and unlisting, **0 ETH is stranded** in every scenario.

These are model estimates, not guarantees.

## Why each part of the rule exists

| Alternative | Scenario | What goes wrong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No limit on unsettled draws | bursts of 20 | 403 of ~2,360 draws refunded for slippage per run: each refunded player loses the VRF fee and gas |
| Fill measured by prize count | keeper every 10 steps | NFT odds reach ×2.19 design (weight-based: ×1.52): draws take mostly the heaviest, smallest prizes |
| No withdrawal reserve | creator withdraws everything | the pool never finishes stocking up, so purchases never open |
| Reserve without the spare top prize | creator withdraws everything | the largest prize is missing 57% of the time (with the spare: 9%) |
| Keeper gas paid from the creator's ETH (before DECISIONS 51) | gas rises 1 → 12 gwei | −1.44 mETH per draw for the creator: the pool kept paying keeper gas beyond what its markup earned |

## Findings for the owner

1. **Keepers cannot cost more than the markup.** At high gas they wait; inventory runs down and purchases pause (gas
   rising to 12 gwei: admitted 11% of the time, keepers took 0.167 of 0.172 ETH markup).
2. **The creator can still end slightly below zero** when keepers take nearly all the markup, because the opening
   stock-up gas (about 0.027 ETH at 1 gwei) and the mint fees of prizes listed at close are fixed costs the markup must
   recover (gas rising to 12 gwei: −0.09 ETH over 133 draws; markup at the 10% floor: −0.04 mETH per draw).
3. **The mint fee can rise to 0.01 ETH** (Shapes admin `setMintFee`, capped at one unit). Purchases then pause until
   the creator saves the design again at a markup that covers the new fee (0.005 ETH needs 50% with 0.01 Shapes).
4. **A late keeper pauses purchases instead of drifting the odds.** With a keeper every 10 steps, purchases are admitted
   27% of the time and NFT odds stay within ×1.52 of the design.

Creator net includes the creator's own gas for the unwind calls.
