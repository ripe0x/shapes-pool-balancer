# Simulation results

Generated by `npm run sim` (`sim/flows.ts`), 40 runs per row, 400 steps each. Pool: one 0.3 ETH NFT (a fresh one is
listed when it is won) and the Balanced mix at 20 small Shapes (20 × 0.01, 10 × 0.05, 2 × 0.1, 1 × 0.5 ETH Shapes and
7 × 0.02 ETH packs), 15% markup, 0.001 ETH mint fee, 1 gwei basefee, 3 draws a step, keeper every step, 0.2 ETH spare
deposit. Upkeep as on chain: 5 reconciles, 5 Shape units and 4 sizes per call, smallest value first. FWA is modelled
from its code: weights 1e36/price, fee = EV × 1.05, owner proceeds = EV at request, staged listings activated 6 per
request, 10% slippage refunds.

**Money flows.** After every step the ledger reconciles to the wei: creator deposits + NFTs listed + player payments =
pool ETH + FWA owner proceeds + FWA escrow + refunds owed + listed prize backing + prizes won + NFTs won + mint fees +
FWA edge + VRF fees + keeper rewards + creator withdrawals. After closing and unlisting, **0 ETH is stranded** in every
scenario.

These are model estimates, not guarantees.

## Why each part of the rule exists

| Alternative | Scenario | What goes wrong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No limit on unsettled draws | bursts of 20 | 669 of ~3,240 draws refunded for slippage per run: each refunded player loses the VRF fee and gas |
| Fill measured by prize count | keeper every 10 steps | NFT odds reach ×2.19 design (weight-based: ×1.52): draws take mostly the heaviest, smallest prizes |
| No withdrawal reserve | creator withdraws everything | restocking stalls: purchases admitted 6% of the time, the largest prize always missing |
| Reserve without the spare top prize | creator withdraws everything | the largest prize is missing 52% of the time (with the spare: 10%) |

## Findings for the owner

1. **Gas is the main profit risk.** The creator pays keeper gas (capped at 5 gwei). Expected net per draw for the
   default Balanced pool at 15% markup (`economics()`): +0.49 mETH at 1 gwei with 3 draws per upkeep, −0.10 mETH at
   2 gwei, −1.75 mETH at 5 gwei. With one draw per upkeep it turns negative near 0.8 gwei. Restocking after several
   draws at once halves the cost per draw.
2. **The mint fee can rise to 0.01 ETH** (Shapes admin `setMintFee`, capped at one unit). Purchases then pause until
   the creator saves the design again, accepting the new fee.
3. **A late keeper pauses purchases instead of drifting the odds.** With a keeper every 10 steps, purchases are admitted
   28% of the time and NFT odds stay within ×1.52 of the design.
