# Reserve redesign: capital and operating scenarios

Generated by `npm run sim`. **Model-only gas, demand, selection and timing assumptions.** Contract parameter fixtures and separate real-fork money replay establish arithmetic/branch correspondence, not these demand or availability forecasts.

**Corrected batch/VRF model:** this generation supersedes the earlier freshly-quoted-per-request batch assumption. Purchase batches snapshot one price, later positive drift can refund requests, and every accepted request still incurs the separately reported purchaser VRF fee. Prior outputs remain in Git history. The controlled five-word fork comparison establishes that branch only; full keeper gas/profit, cash, concurrency and demand are not calibrated forecasts.

**Funding-quote parity:** Every current opening/stocked value in the 40-row capital matrix is exported from actual `KeeperPolicyCalls.reserve` calls on fork block 26,382,349, using the real Shapes mint fee of 0.001 ETH and the deployed V1 maximum. The non-view probe asserts the selected basefee and bid inside execution. `shared/reserve-capital.test.ts` matches every current report row to `test/fixtures/reserve-capital.json`, plus closed-outstanding and paid-disabled-deficit cases. This validates funding quotes, not forty separately executed pool lifecycles.

All amounts are ETH; 0.001 ETH mint fee, 15% Shape markup, FWA 5% edge. Gas columns are basefees; bids add 0.05 gwei except at 5 gwei where the bid is 5 gwei. Opening capital includes initial backing, mint fees, bounded current setup and one working buffer. Stocked reserve excludes backing already in Shapes. No ordinary operating top-ups occur. Only the explicitly named policy-adoption scenario deposits its quoted activation shortfall. Curated NFTs are not automatically replaced, except in the explicitly named creator-replacement scenario. Creator claims after every maintenance batch.

## Operating scenarios

Each scenario is 30 days with baseline demand of 40 draws/day unless named otherwise; elasticity changes desired volume after price changes, deterministic seed 12345. A lower request count reflects refusal/curated-NFT depletion, not manufactured demand. Partial replenishment uses only operating ETH after excluding booked creator profit and reserving the current call’s immutable payment ceiling. It stops at the first unaffordable prize in value order. Admission and one acquisition quote are checked once per purchase batch. Each subsequent canonical selection uses the request price and the unchanged 10% positive-slippage refund guard; it does not buy a freshly quoted draw. The model accepts maximum negative slippage, like the controlled fork comparison. Missing inventory can coexist with open admission; a paused pool cannot bootstrap new revenue from refused purchases.

## Money destinations and demand limits

Every scenario asserts pool deposits plus actually collected FWA owner proceeds equal liquid pool ETH plus held Shape/Pack backing plus creator claims plus delivered backing plus Shapes mint fees plus keeper transfers. Contributed curated-NFT value equals retained plus delivered value; automatic replacement is disabled by default. Uncollected FWA proceeds remain outside pool liquid ETH. Paid work never consumes booked profit. Claimable surplus is the greater of booked profit and balance above the current reserve, spent profit first.

A 20% draw-price increase implies 83.3%, 69.4% or 57.9% retained demand at hypothetical elasticity 1, 2 or 3. These sensitivities are not empirical estimates. Higher surcharges cannot be assumed harmless. The V1 surcharge and creator markup allocation are unchanged by reserve reduction.

## Things that don't make sense

A fully backed prize board can still have no operating liquidity. Once the weighted inventory or curated-NFT gate stops purchases, future demand cannot replenish it; a creator must contribute ETH/NFTs, lower the target, or close. These are explicit operating conditions, not assets missing from purchasers. Model timing and gas still need real-fork calibration; a balanced ledger alone cannot validate sustainability.
