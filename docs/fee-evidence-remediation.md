# Request-board fee and creator-entitlement evidence

This change repairs audit findings E2/E7 in the economic evidence. It changes no restocking charge, keeper quote, creator entitlement or numeric outcome threshold. The owner retained approximate current-board allocation and concurrent purchases in R55–R58; E1 is quantified rather than claimed fixed. No serialization or forced zero-profit allocation is introduced.

FWA prices a request from its weighted board average and credits that fixed base only when delivery succeeds. The surcharge attached to the selected prize is an inventory statistic. It is not the surcharge cash realized by that purchase. `FlowBreakdown.t.sol` now captures the actual board after `acquireBatch` has performed any idle staging activation, separates the weighted non-surcharge price, and defines the surcharge component as the actual request base less that non-surcharge component. Integer division assigns at most one wei of the decomposition's rounding residue to surcharge. Each fulfilled request is checked against the real FWA `OwnerProceedsAccrued` event; all fulfilled bases must equal actual collected plus still-uncollected FWA owner proceeds. Pending and refunded acquisitions contribute no realized surcharge. No future unsold fee is treated as liquid ETH.

The selected listing surcharge remains separately reported as `selected_listing_restock_fees_won_wei` and remains useful for reconciling outstanding listing fees. `restock_fee_receipts_wei`, keeper coverage and the flow-return column use actual realized request-board surcharge instead. A positive one-draw selected-fee margin can therefore coexist with a negative cash fee/pay margin. Aggregate demand and adverse outcomes still determine sustainability; this repair adds no tax and makes no volume forecast.

The creator split comparison no longer skips zero bookings. It records overbooking and underbooking separately and uses their sum, `split_absolute_error_wei`, for the historical O9 configuration with its existing 0.001 ETH bound. The new concurrent adversarial cases still exceed that threshold; they report the actual error rather than relaxing it. General owner acceptance of bounded approximation does not supply a new numerical ceiling or outcome signature. Opposite errors cannot cancel. The reached in-flight and policy-switch comparisons require positive entitlement; zero allocation when a positive amount is owed fails rather than passing O8's upper bound alone. The old O9 wording gated only overbooking, and the old O11 wording described fees in won prizes as repayments; those historical declarations remain in Git and the supplied audit.

## Things that don't make sense

A keeper-coverage claim based on the winning prize's fee answers a different question from actual FWA cash income. A profit upper bound that accepts zero cannot establish that the creator received a positive entitlement. These evidence defects are repaired here; release claims still depend on the final production candidate and affected runs.
